Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This page covers the Badboons game for Meta Quest, made by Alan Nordyke. It applies to everything in the game, including monsters, maps and modes that get added in later updates. If an update ever needs to use your information in a new way, this page gets changed before that update comes out.

Badboons has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn't have its own account system. It never asks for your email, your real name, your location or your contacts, and nothing about you is sold or shared.

What stays on your headset

Your save stays in the game's own storage on your Quest: your level, Banana Rations, cosmetics, fur colour, the name you picked, your settings, your block list and a record of what you've bought (purchase IDs and whether you own the VIP Pack). There's also a small performance log the game uses to find slow spots. The game doesn't send any of that anywhere. If Cloud Backup is turned on in your headset's settings, Meta keeps a backup copy of the save so it comes back if you reinstall or move to a new headset.

What gets sent when you play online

Online rooms run through Photon (Exit Games GmbH), the multiplayer service the game uses. While you're in a room, your headset sends what the other players need to see you: where you are, your hands, the name you picked, how your monkey looks, and things that happen in the round, like grabs. Photon's servers see your device's IP address while you're connected, the same as any online service. None of this is stored by us. Photon's own policy is at photonengine.com/privacy.

If you let the game use your microphone, your voice goes live to the other players in your room through Photon. It isn't recorded or saved. If you say no to the microphone, everything else still works and your voice just isn't sent. The name you pick is shown to other players, so it's a good idea not to use your real name.

Your Meta account

The game asks Meta's platform on your headset for your Meta user ID. It's used to check that the game was installed from the Meta Horizon Store, to keep blocks working even if someone changes their in-game name, and to make sure a report points at the right person. The games of the other players in your room receive it for those reasons. It's never shown to anyone or written to a log, and it's only stored if a player blocks you, in that player's block list on their own headset. The store check is a yes-or-no answer when the game starts, and nothing from it is kept.

Muting, blocking and reporting

Muting or blocking someone only changes what you see and hear. It doesn't do anything to them, and your block list stays on your headset.

If you report a player, the game opens Meta's own report form, where you pick who to report and why. Meta looks at the report and acts on it under Meta's rules. To help with that, the game adds a few details about the round: the room code, the in-game names of the players in the room, the game's version and the time.

Buying Banana Rations and the VIP Pack

Badboons is free. If you want, you can buy packs of Banana Rations (the in-game currency) or the VIP Pack through the Meta Horizon Store's own checkout. Meta handles the payment, so we never see your card or payment details. The game only gets told what was bought, so it can add it to your save.

Bought rations go into your save, which Cloud Backup can restore (see above). The VIP Pack is a one-time purchase that Meta keeps in your purchase history, so it comes back on its own after a reinstall or on a new headset.

Meta

Your Meta account, your purchases and your headset's system features are handled by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy: meta.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Age

Badboons is a horror game with blood and gore, made for teens and adults. Check the store's age rating. It isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Since the game keeps your save on your headset, uninstalling Badboons deletes it (Library, then the game's menu, then Uninstall). If Cloud Backup is on, Meta also keeps a backup copy, which you can manage or delete in your Meta account's settings. We don't keep a copy, so there's nothing on our end to delete. Meta keeps its own records of purchases and reports under its policies.
