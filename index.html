Badboons

A multiplayer horror game for Meta Quest. One player is the monster, and everyone else tries to survive until time runs out. Free in Early Access.

About the game

You move with your arms, the same way as in Gorilla Tag. Each round, one player plays as the monster and the others hide, run and climb to stay alive.

The first monster is a sand monster. It can grab players and tear them apart, and it can turn into sand to get through small gaps and pipes. The first map is the Backrooms.

Between rounds there's a hub with a shop, where you can spend Banana Rations (the in-game currency) on cosmetics.

Early Access

Badboons is still being made. More monsters, maps and modes are planned, and the game will change with updates. Feedback and ideas are welcome at noice787965@gmail.com.
